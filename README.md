Entry for the make.fun competition **u-suroso-69-one-sentence-absurd-invention-pitch-5326c34a** — "One-Sentence Absurd Invention Pitch" (Growth, $2.50 USDC top 10, free entry). The program writes one sentence, exactly 146 characters, to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The pitch

> A car indicator that lets out a tiny polite cough whenever the driver ahead forgets to use theirs, restoring motorway manners one cough at a time.

Why this one, after drafting 26 candidates and judging them pairwise against the brief (funny first, then clever fix, original, tight):

- **Funny from the invention itself.** The laugh is the indicator's tiny polite cough — a car clearing its throat at another car — not a joke bolted on at the end.
- **Clever fix, silly yet weirdly plausible.** It shames the forgetful driver the way a pointed cough shames a queue-jumper, restoring manners one cough at a time instead of honking.
- **Original.** A scan of all 45 entries on the board at build time showed no car, indicator or cough anywhere in the field — while toasters (7 entries, including two parachute toasters), apologising appliances, microwaves, toothbrushes and sock drawers were all taken.
- **Tight.** One clean sentence naming the annoyance (the driver ahead forgetting to indicate), the invention (the coughing indicator) and its silly mechanism, with nothing left over.

Runner-up: a shower curtain that clings to the wall instead of your leg, having finally learned something about personal space.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt   # the pitch, exactly 146 characters, no newline
```

`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` overrides the output directory (default `/output`). No network, no randomness, no dependencies — the same run always writes the same 146 bytes.
